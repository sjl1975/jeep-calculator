Verification policy — important shock torque discrepancies

Front and rear shock torque values CONFLICT across published JL/JT secondary references. They are intentionally marked unverified until checked against the applicable factory service procedure. Apex torque values are from the uploaded Apex manual. MetalCloak's uploaded installation manual provides tool sizes but generally not numerical torque. Published torque references are labeled by source authority. Some are aftermarket reference charts rather than primary OEM service documents; cross-check these before service. Missing values remain explicitly unverified. Verify exact hardware, model applicability, and current instructions before tightening safety-critical fasteners. Aftermarket fasteners can supersede OEM torque values. Torque-plus-angle steps must be followed exactly; never treat the first torque value as the final specification. Confirm single-use hardware and torque procedures.

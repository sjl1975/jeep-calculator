Verification policy

Published torque references are labeled by source authority. Some are aftermarket reference charts rather than primary OEM service documents; cross-check these before service. Missing values remain explicitly unverified. Verify exact hardware, model applicability, and current instructions before tightening safety-critical fasteners. Aftermarket fasteners can supersede OEM torque values. Torque-plus-angle steps must be followed exactly; never treat the first torque value as the final specification. Confirm single-use hardware and torque procedures.

Coverage roadmap

Database structure supports JL 2018–2026 and JT 2020–2026 model-year selection, engine, trim, axle, manufacturer, and system. Additional model years, parts, and configurations require individually verified sources before being populated. Year selection is a filter, not a claim that every year has been researched.

Torque source policy — manufacturer first

When a component is selected under a specific aftermarket manufacturer, use that manufacturer's published specification for the exact product and supplied hardware, even when an OEM or third-party reference differs. Do not suppress a manufacturer's specified torque merely because another source disagrees. For reused factory bolts, the manufacturer's installation instructions may defer to OEM torque procedures; when the supplied installation manual gives no numeric torque, identify the missing value rather than implying the manual provided one. OEM entries remain separate and require their own applicable factory-service verification. Follow torque-plus-angle procedures, hardware replacement and installation notes exactly. MetalCloak's uploaded RockSport installation guide specifies many socket sizes but does not state numeric shock torque; separate MetalCloak published JL torque-chart values are labeled as such, not attributed to the uploaded guide. JT fitment and different supplied hardware must be verified independently.

Coverage roadmap

Database structure supports JL 2018–2026 and JT 2020–2026 model-year selection, engine, trim, axle, manufacturer, and system. Additional model years, parts, and configurations require individually verified sources before being populated. Year selection is a filter, not a claim that every year has been researched.

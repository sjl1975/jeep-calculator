Select the actual installed joint arrangement. The factory CV joint is not treated as a conventional single U-joint.

Measured angles

Sign convention: looking from the side, an axis that rises toward the FRONT of the Jeep is positive (+); one that falls toward the front is negative (−). All readings are degrees relative to horizontal. Measure shaft centerlines, not arbitrary cast surfaces.

A yoke/flange face perpendicular to the shaft requires a 90° conversion and the correct sign. Do not enter the face angle directly.

Front caster

No factory housing-flat offset is assumed. Verify this exact surface's calibration before using the result.

Joint screening
